About Amy

Amy Istueta Shelley

Holistic Health & Stress Consultant · Director of Stress Mind Institute, USA

Amy Istueta Shelley holds a Bachelor of Science (USA) and international certifications in Stress Management (University of Massachusetts), Clinical Hypnotherapy — CHt (IACT, USA), and Neuro-Mind Life Coaching (ICCI). She combines evidence-based approaches with complementary wellness modalities to support emotional regulation, resilience, and personal growth. Before moving into stress management, she was a tenured History professor at the University of Buenos Aires.

My work isn’t about giving you one more technique to add to your list — it’s about helping you understand your own stress and build, step by step, the tools that bring back calm and clarity. [to be confirmed with Amy]
